Privacy Policy

DailyMeds for iPhone · Last updated: September 6, 2026

The short version: DailyMeds doesn't know who you are. Your data never leaves your phone. That's not a feature, it's just how the app works.

Who we are

DailyMeds is an indie app built by figoodle. If you email support@figoodle.com, a real person reads it.

Data we collect

None. There's no account, no backend, no analytics. Every medication you add, every dose you log, every schedule you set, it all lives in a local database on your iPhone. We have no server that receives it and no way to access it.

Permissions

  • Camera. Only used in the Scan tab. You point it at a box, Apple's Vision framework reads the text on-device, the camera closes. No image is saved or sent anywhere. Never runs in the background.
  • Notifications. Used to remind you to take your medication at the times you set. All reminders are scheduled locally. There's no push notification server involved.
  • Apple Health (iOS 26 and later). If you tap "Import from Apple Health" in Settings, the app requests read-only access to import your medications. Nothing is ever written back to Health. Skip it and everything else works fine.
  • Preferences. Your settings are saved using iOS's standard local storage. We can't read them.

Security and storage

All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData, protected by iOS's standard data-at-rest encryption. It's as secure as everything else on your iPhone, tied to your device passcode and Face ID/Touch ID.

You can export your data from Settings any time as a backup file. That file goes through iOS's share sheet to wherever you send it and we never receive a copy. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a Mac, DailyMeds data is part of that backup under Apple's encryption. We have no access to iCloud backups.

Third-party code

None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ad framework. Just Apple's own frameworks. This was a deliberate choice, not a coincidence.

Advertising and tracking

DailyMeds has no ads and doesn't use your Advertising Identifier. It has no idea what other apps you use and makes no attempt to find out.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings inside the app and tap Delete All Data. It's instant and permanent. Alternatively, delete the app for the same result. There's nothing on our end to delete because we never had any of it.

Children

DailyMeds doesn't collect data from anyone. There's nothing age-sensitive about an app that only stores data locally, so COPPA and similar laws don't create any obligations for us beyond what we already do.

Medical disclaimer

DailyMeds is a reminder app, not a medical device. It helps you remember to take medication your doctor already prescribed. It doesn't diagnose, advise, or flag interactions. If something seems wrong with your medication, talk to a pharmacist or your doctor, not an app.

Changes

We'll update the date at the top of this page if anything changes. We don't have a way to reach you directly, so this page is the only record. We won't change anything unless something about the app actually changes.

Questions
