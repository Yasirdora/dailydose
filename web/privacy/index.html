Privacy Policy

DailyMeds for iPhone · Last updated: September 6, 2026

DailyMeds is private by design. Your medications, schedules, and history never leave your device. There is no account, no server, and no tracking of any kind.

Who we are

DailyMeds is developed and published by figoodle. You can reach us at support@figoodle.com.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data.

Everything you enter into DailyMeds — medication names, doses, schedules, notes, and your dose history — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's on-device storage. It is never transmitted to us or any third party, and we have no technical ability to access it.

Device permissions the app uses

  • Camera — used only when you open the Scan tab to read text from a medication label or box using on-device text recognition (Apple's Vision framework). No image is captured, saved, or sent anywhere. The camera is never accessed in the background.
  • Notifications — used to deliver local medication reminders at the times you set. All notifications are created and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification server is involved.
  • Apple Health (iOS 26 and later) — if you choose to import medications from the Apple Health app, DailyMeds requests read-only access to your medications. Nothing is ever written back to Apple Health. This feature is entirely opt-in and requires your explicit permission via Apple's standard Health authorization prompt.
  • App preferences — reminder and tracking settings are stored in your device's standard local preferences (UserDefaults). They are not synced, shared, or accessible to us.

Data storage and security

All app data is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS's built-in data protection, which encrypts data at rest using your device passcode. We do not operate any servers or databases that hold your information.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a Mac or PC, DailyMeds data is included in that device backup under Apple's own encryption. We do not control or have access to iCloud backups.

Data sharing and third parties

We do not share your data with anyone — because we never receive it in the first place. DailyMeds contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, crash reporting tools, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks of any kind. The app uses only Apple's own system frameworks.

Backups and data portability

DailyMeds lets you export a full backup of your medications and history from Settings at any time. The backup file is generated on your device and shared only through iOS's standard share sheet — to wherever you choose to send it. We never receive a copy of this file.

You can restore a backup on the same device or a new one by importing the file in Settings.

Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on our end. Your data is entirely under your control at all times.

To delete your data from the app, open Settings inside DailyMeds and tap Delete All Data. This immediately removes all medications and history from your device and cannot be undone. Deleting the app achieves the same result.

Advertising and tracking

DailyMeds contains no advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not build profiles, sell data, or use your information for any commercial purpose. The app does not request access to your Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

Children's privacy

DailyMeds does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Because no data is collected at all, the app does not require any special handling under children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) or equivalent regulations elsewhere.

Medical disclaimer

DailyMeds is a personal reminder and tracking tool, not a regulated medical device. It helps you keep track of medication that has already been prescribed to you by a healthcare professional. It does not provide medical advice, suggest diagnoses, check for drug interactions, or replace the guidance of a doctor or pharmacist. Always follow the instructions given by your healthcare provider.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Because we have no way to contact you directly, we recommend bookmarking this page if you wish to be notified of future changes.
