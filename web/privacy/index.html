Privacy Policy

DailyMeds for iPhone · Last updated: September 2026

DailyMeds is private by design. Your medications, schedules, and history never leave your device. There is no account, no server, and no tracking of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing.

Every piece of information you enter into DailyMeds — medication names, doses, schedules, and your history — is stored locally on your device. It is never transmitted to any server, never read by us, and never shared with any third party.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — used only when you open the Scan tab to read a medication label using on-device text recognition. No image is saved, uploaded, or processed outside the app. Access is requested only when you choose to use that feature.
  • Notifications — used to send you local medication reminders at the times you set. All notifications are scheduled on your device. None are sent from any server.
  • Apple Health (iOS 26 and later) — if you choose to import medications from the Health app, DailyMeds reads those medications with your explicit permission. Nothing is ever written back to Apple Health. Entirely opt-in.
  • App preferences — your reminder and tracking settings are stored in your device's standard preferences storage. They stay on your device.

Backups and data export

DailyMeds lets you export a backup file and import it again on the same or a new device. That file is generated on your device and shared only through iOS's standard share sheet. We never see it, receive it, or store it.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, DailyMeds data is included in that system backup under Apple's standard encryption.

Third-party services

DailyMeds uses no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, crash reporters, advertising networks, or tracking tools of any kind. There is no external code in the app beyond Apple's own frameworks.

Advertising and tracking

We do not advertise within the app. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell, share, or rent your information. We have nothing to sell because we collect nothing.

Children's privacy

DailyMeds does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected at all, the app is appropriate for any age group.

Medical disclaimer

DailyMeds is a reminder tool, not a medical device. It helps you keep track of medication that has already been prescribed to you. It does not provide medical advice, check for drug interactions, or replace the guidance of a doctor or pharmacist. Always follow the instructions given by your healthcare provider.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device. To remove everything, open Settings inside the app and tap "Delete All Data." You can also simply delete the app, which removes all associated data along with it.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Because we have no way to contact you directly, you can bookmark this page to check for updates at any time.
