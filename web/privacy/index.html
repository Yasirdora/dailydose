Privacy Policy

DailyMeds for iPhone · Last updated: September 6, 2026

Short version: DailyMeds doesn't know anything about you. Your medications stay on your phone. That's the whole point.

Who made this app

DailyMeds is built by figoodle. It's a one-person indie app, not a company with a privacy team and a legal department. If you have a question, you're emailing a real person at support@figoodle.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Genuinely.

There's no account, no sign-in, no server receiving your data. The medications you add, the doses you log, the schedules you set — all of it lives in SwiftData on your iPhone and goes nowhere. We don't see it, can't see it, and have no infrastructure to receive it even if we wanted to.

What the app asks permission for

  • Camera — only if you use the label scanner. You open Scan, point it at your medication box, and the app reads the text off the packaging using Apple's Vision framework on your device. No photo is taken, no image leaves your phone. The camera is never used in the background.
  • Notifications — so the app can remind you to take your medication. Every reminder is scheduled locally on your device using iOS's notification system. There's no server sending you push notifications.
  • Apple Health (iOS 26+) — only if you tap "Import from Apple Health" in Settings. The app asks to read your medications from Health so you don't have to type them in manually. It reads and imports — nothing is ever written back. You can decline and the rest of the app works fine.
  • Preferences storage — your settings (reminder behaviour, dose window, etc.) are saved using iOS's standard UserDefaults. They stay on your device and we can't read them.

Your data, your device

Because everything is stored locally, you're in complete control. There's no account to deactivate, no server-side record to request deletion of.

To wipe everything: open Settings inside the app and tap Delete All Data. It's immediate and can't be undone — so export a backup first if you might want it later. Deleting the app itself does the same thing.

You can export a backup of your medications and history from Settings any time. It's a file that goes through iOS's share sheet to wherever you send it. We never see it.

Backups and iCloud

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or your Mac, DailyMeds data is included in that backup the same way any other app's data is — encrypted by Apple, managed by you. We have no access to it.

Third-party code

There isn't any. No analytics SDK, no crash reporter phoning home, no advertising framework, no tracking library. The app uses Apple's frameworks and nothing else. This wasn't a budget decision — it's the design.

Advertising and tracking

DailyMeds has no ads. It doesn't use your Advertising Identifier (IDFA). It doesn't track you across other apps. It has no idea what other apps you use.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone — children or adults. There's nothing age-gated, nothing that requires an age check, and nothing we'd need to handle differently for someone under 13. COPPA and equivalent laws in other countries simply don't apply because the data collection threshold is zero.

A note on medical use

DailyMeds is a reminder app. It helps you keep track of medication your doctor already prescribed. It doesn't give advice, suggest diagnoses, or flag drug interactions. Please don't use it as a substitute for talking to your doctor or pharmacist — it's a tool for remembering, not for deciding.

If this policy changes

We'll update the date at the top of this page. Since we have no way to reach you — no email, no account — checking back here is the only way to see changes. We'll only make changes if something genuinely changes about how the app works, not just to update legal boilerplate.
