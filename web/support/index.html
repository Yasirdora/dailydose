DailyMeds for iPhone

Common questions

How do I add a medication?

Open the Meds tab and tap the + button in the top right corner. You can type the name and schedule manually, or tap Scan Medication to point the camera at the label or box and let the app fill in the details automatically.

My notifications stopped arriving. What's wrong?

Go to Settings inside the app (tap the gear icon on the Doses tab). If there is a red banner at the top saying "Reminders are off in iOS Settings", tap it — it will take you directly to the right place in your iPhone settings to turn them back on. If notifications are on but you're still missing reminders, make sure Background App Refresh is enabled for DailyMeds in iPhone Settings.

Can I log a dose I already took earlier?

Yes. On the Doses tab, use the week strip at the top to go back to the day in question. Tap the medication card, then tap Taken. The log records the time you actually tapped it, so it will show as late if the scheduled time has passed.

How do I track how many pills I have left?

Open the medication editor (tap the medication in the Meds tab, then tap Edit). Turn on Track supply and enter your current count. DailyMeds counts down with each logged dose and sends a refill reminder when you reach the threshold you set.

How do I back up my medications and history?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Export backup. A file is created and the iOS share sheet appears — you can save it to Files, send it to yourself by email, or save it anywhere you like. To restore, tap Import backup in the same Settings screen and pick the file.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

The easiest way is to restore from an iPhone backup (iCloud or Mac/PC) — DailyMeds data is included automatically. Alternatively, export a backup from Settings on your old phone, AirDrop or email it to your new phone, then import it from Settings on the new device.

How do I delete a medication?

Open the Meds tab, tap the medication, then tap Edit. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Medication. Your dose history for that medication is kept in your records even after deletion.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings inside the app, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete All Data. This removes every medication and every history entry from your device immediately and cannot be undone. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

Does DailyMeds work without an internet connection?

Yes, completely. DailyMeds has no server and makes no network requests. Everything runs on your device, so it works fully offline at all times.

What does the dose window setting do?

The dose window is how long after a scheduled time a dose can still be logged as "taken on time". Once that window closes, the dose counts as missed in your history. You can adjust the window in Settings → Tracking → Dose Window.
